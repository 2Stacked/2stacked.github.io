PropertyLedger is made by 2Stacked Development. If you have a question about this policy, write to
[{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}).

## In short

2Stacked Development collects no data from PropertyLedger. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
no tracking and no third-party code. It makes no network requests of its own.

## Where your data lives

Everything you add to PropertyLedger is stored on your device, in the app's own storage. The home-screen widget
shows a summary that the app saves on the same device.

## iCloud sync (optional)

iCloud sync is off until you turn it on in the app's Settings. When it's on, your records and photos sync through
Apple's iCloud to your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account, so they appear on your other devices.
2Stacked Development can't see that data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Camera and photos

PropertyLedger uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to attach a picture to a record. The
picture is stored with that record. The app doesn't look at the rest of your photo library.

## Contacts

When you pick a contact to add a person, only the details of the contact you picked are copied into the app.
PropertyLedger doesn't read your contact list.

## Files

Documents you attach are copied into the app and stored with the record.

## Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Sharing

Your Home Report PDF, photos and files leave the app only when you share them with the share sheet, and only to the
place you choose.

## Deleting your data

Delete records in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on that device. If iCloud sync is on, also remove
PropertyLedger's data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

## Children

PropertyLedger isn't directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
