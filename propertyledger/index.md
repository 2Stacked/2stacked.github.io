**{{ app.tagline.en }}**

PropertyLedger keeps your home's whole story in one place: the things in it, the people who work on it, what it
has cost, and everything that has happened to it. It's a record you could hand to a buyer, an insurer or the next
owner.

- **Items and spaces**: appliances, systems and furniture, room by room, with photos, warranties and paint colours.
- **Documents**: receipts, warranties, invoices, manuals and insurance, with photos or files attached.
- **People**: the contractors and service providers who work on your home, and their quotes.
- **Spending and projects**: what each item, repair and renovation has cost.
- **Timeline**: every change to your home's record, by date.
- **Home Report**: your home's whole record as a PDF to share.

Everything stays on your iPhone. You can choose to sync it through your own iCloud account. There are no accounts
and no ads, and nothing is collected. See the [privacy policy](/propertyledger/privacy/).
